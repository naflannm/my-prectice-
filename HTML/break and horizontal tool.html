Short poems:

Weeping

by Federico Gareaa Larea


Weeping,
I do down the street
grotesque, Without solution
With the sadness of cyrano
And Quixote.

String Bass

by Adrian Green


some like to dominate,
others creers
a voluptuos rhythm
on pliant string,
this pulse drives life
through wanton counterpoint,
the heart and harmony
of things.
